Our Heritage

The Healing City of the Uva Highlands

Nestled at an elevation of 1,230 meters, Bandarawela boasts a unique, mild, and relatively dry climate. During the colonial era, the British established the town as an exclusive health resort and sanatorium to escape the tropical heat.

Today, the town is a thriving hub surrounded by lush, world-renowned tea plantations and breathtaking waterfalls.

  • World-class Ceylon tea estates
  • Historic 19th-century railway network
  • Ancient Buddhist archaeology (Dowa Temple)
